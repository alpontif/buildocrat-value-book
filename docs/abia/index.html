About ₦1.6m a year for a 3-bedroom flat, ₦989,000 for a 2-bedroom flat and ₦639,000 for a 1-bedroom or mini flat (October 2026).

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Umuahia, Abia?

Asking prices for a 4-bedroom house in Umuahia, Abia are around ₦62.3m; a 3-bedroom flat around ₦55.4m (October 2026).
