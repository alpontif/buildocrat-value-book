Questions people ask

How much is a plot of land in Minna, Niger?

A typical 600 m² residential plot in Minna, Niger is about ₦7.3m (around ₦12,200 per m²) in asking prices as of October 2026. Commercial land is about ₦17,500 per m². Source: Buildocrat Land Value Book, updated weekly.

How much is rent for a 3-bedroom flat in Minna, Niger?

About ₦1.5m a year for a 3-bedroom flat, ₦1.1m for a 2-bedroom flat and ₦490,000 for a 1-bedroom or mini flat (October 2026).

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Minna, Niger?

Asking prices for a 4-bedroom house in Minna, Niger are around ₦63.9m; a 3-bedroom flat around ₦43.4m (October 2026).

What is the price of farmland in Minna, Niger?

Farmland in Minna, Niger is about ₦7.4m per hectare (October 2026).
