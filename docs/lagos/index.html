Questions people ask

How much is a plot of land in Ikeja, Lagos?

A typical 600 m² residential plot in Ikeja, Lagos is about ₦175.8m (around ₦293,000 per m²) in asking prices as of October 2026. Commercial land is about ₦387,000 per m². Source: Buildocrat Land Value Book, updated weekly.

How much is rent for a 3-bedroom flat in Ikeja, Lagos?

About ₦5.3m a year for a 3-bedroom flat, ₦3.4m for a 2-bedroom flat and ₦1.7m for a 1-bedroom or mini flat (October 2026).

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Ikeja, Lagos?

Asking prices for a 4-bedroom house in Ikeja, Lagos are around ₦286m; a 3-bedroom flat around ₦179m (October 2026).

What is the price of farmland in Ikeja, Lagos?

Farmland in Ikeja, Lagos is about ₦73.3m per hectare (October 2026).
