How much is a plot of land in Abakaliki, Ebonyi?

A typical 600 m² residential plot in Abakaliki, Ebonyi is about ₦6.1m (around ₦10,200 per m²) in asking prices as of October 2026. Commercial land is about ₦19,800 per m². Source: Buildocrat Land Value Book, updated weekly.

How much is rent for a 3-bedroom flat in Abakaliki, Ebonyi?

About ₦1.2m a year for a 3-bedroom flat, ₦991,000 for a 2-bedroom flat and ₦500,000 for a 1-bedroom or mini flat (October 2026).

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Abakaliki, Ebonyi?

Asking prices for a 4-bedroom house in Abakaliki, Ebonyi are around ₦59.7m; a 3-bedroom flat around ₦45.7m (October 2026).

What is the price of farmland in Abakaliki, Ebonyi?
