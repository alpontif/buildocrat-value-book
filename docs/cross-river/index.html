Questions people ask

How much is a plot of land in Calabar, Cross River?

A typical 600 m² residential plot in Calabar, Cross River is about ₦10.1m (around ₦16,900 per m²) in asking prices as of October 2026. Commercial land is about ₦32,800 per m². Source: Buildocrat Land Value Book, updated weekly.

How much is rent for a 3-bedroom flat in Calabar, Cross River?

About ₦2m a year for a 3-bedroom flat, ₦1m for a 2-bedroom flat and ₦476,000 for a 1-bedroom or mini flat (October 2026).

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Calabar, Cross River?

Asking prices for a 4-bedroom house in Calabar, Cross River are around ₦104m; a 3-bedroom flat around ₦54.9m (October 2026).

What is the price of farmland in Calabar, Cross River?

Farmland in Calabar, Cross River is about ₦6.7m per hectare (October 2026).
