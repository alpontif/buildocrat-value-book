How much is a plot of land in Calabar, Cross River?

How much is rent for a 3-bedroom flat in Calabar, Cross River?

About ₦1.7m a year for a 3-bedroom flat, ₦1m for a 2-bedroom flat and ₦424,000 for a 1-bedroom or mini flat (October 2026).

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Calabar, Cross River?

Asking prices for a 4-bedroom house in Calabar, Cross River are around ₦92.5m; a 3-bedroom flat around ₦70m (October 2026).
