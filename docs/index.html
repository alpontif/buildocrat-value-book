    How much is a plot of land in Lagos?

    In October 2026, residential land in Lagos runs from about ₦28,900 per m² in rural parts of the state to LGA medians of ₦438,000 in Lagos Mainland, ₦430,000 in Ikeja, ₦378,000 in Lagos Island. A 600 m² plot at the Lagos Mainland median is about ₦262.8m; in outer LGAs such as Ibeju Lekki (₦19.4m), Badagry (₦6.2m), Epe (₦6m) it is far less. See value.buildocrat.store/lagos/ for every LGA.

    How much is a plot of land in Abuja?

    Across the FCT, residential land averages about ₦274,000 per m² near the city, about ₦164.4m for a 600 m² plot, and much more in Maitama, Asokoro and Guzape. See value.buildocrat.store/abuja/.

    Where is the most expensive land in Nigeria?

    The highest residential land values are in Lagos Mainland (Lagos, about ₦438,000 per m²), Ikeja (Lagos, about ₦430,000 per m²), Lagos Island (Lagos, about ₦378,000 per m²), Apapa (Lagos, about ₦371,000 per m²).

    Where is the cheapest land in Nigeria?

    Among state capitals, land is cheapest in Maiduguri (Borno, about ₦1,940 per m²), Damaturu (Yobe, about ₦3,560 per m²), Lokoja (Kogi, about ₦4,070 per m²), Yola (Adamawa, about ₦4,260 per m²), Makurdi (Benue, about ₦4,400 per m²). Village and farmland is cheaper still.

    How do I check the value of a piece of land in Nigeria?

    Open value.buildocrat.store, type the town, estate or coordinates, or click the plot on the satellite map. It shows residential, commercial, industrial and farmland values, house prices, rents and the NIPOST postcode for that spot. For lending, sale or court purposes, commission an ESVARBON-registered valuer.

    How much is farmland per hectare in Nigeria?

    Farmland is typically priced per hectare; state medians in the book run from about ₦155,000 to ₦8.9m per hectare in October 2026, depending on the state and distance to towns.
