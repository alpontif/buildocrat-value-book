Questions people ask

How much is a plot of land in Port Harcourt, Rivers?

A typical 600 m² residential plot in Port Harcourt, Rivers is about ₦42.5m (around ₦70,900 per m²) in asking prices as of October 2026. Commercial land is about ₦107,000 per m². Source: Buildocrat Land Value Book, updated weekly.

How much is rent for a 3-bedroom flat in Port Harcourt, Rivers?

About ₦3.9m a year for a 3-bedroom flat, ₦2.5m for a 2-bedroom flat and ₦1.4m for a 1-bedroom or mini flat (October 2026).

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Port Harcourt, Rivers?

Asking prices for a 4-bedroom house in Port Harcourt, Rivers are around ₦182m; a 3-bedroom flat around ₦151m (October 2026).

What is the price of farmland in Port Harcourt, Rivers?

Farmland in Port Harcourt, Rivers is about ₦21.1m per hectare (October 2026).
