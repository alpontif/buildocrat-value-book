Questions people ask

How much is a plot of land in Abeokuta, Ogun?

A typical 600 m² residential plot in Abeokuta, Ogun is about ₦7m (around ₦11,600 per m²) in asking prices as of October 2026. Commercial land is about ₦14,500 per m². Source: Buildocrat Land Value Book, updated weekly.

How much is rent for a 3-bedroom flat in Abeokuta, Ogun?

About ₦1.6m a year for a 3-bedroom flat, ₦951,000 for a 2-bedroom flat and ₦502,000 for a 1-bedroom or mini flat (October 2026).

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Abeokuta, Ogun?

Asking prices for a 4-bedroom house in Abeokuta, Ogun are around ₦65.9m; a 3-bedroom flat around ₦49.9m (October 2026).

What is the price of farmland in Abeokuta, Ogun?

Farmland in Abeokuta, Ogun is about ₦1.7m per hectare (October 2026).
