Questions people ask

How much is a plot of land in Asaba, Delta?

A typical 600 m² residential plot in Asaba, Delta is about ₦12.1m (around ₦20,200 per m²) in asking prices as of October 2026. Commercial land is about ₦45,200 per m². Source: Buildocrat Land Value Book, updated weekly.

How much is rent for a 3-bedroom flat in Asaba, Delta?

About ₦1.7m a year for a 3-bedroom flat, ₦1.4m for a 2-bedroom flat and ₦482,000 for a 1-bedroom or mini flat (October 2026).

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Asaba, Delta?

Asking prices for a 4-bedroom house in Asaba, Delta are around ₦110m; a 3-bedroom flat around ₦69.6m (October 2026).

What is the price of farmland in Asaba, Delta?

Farmland in Asaba, Delta is about ₦9.2m per hectare (October 2026).
