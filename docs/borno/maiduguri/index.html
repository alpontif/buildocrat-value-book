Land prices in Maiduguri LGA, Borno

Maiduguri is a metro core local government area of Borno (106 km², 1,511 mapped places). Residential land has a median of ₦2,820 per m² (a 900 m² plot about ₦2.5m), from about ₦2,550 per m² in its villages to ₦3,400 in its most valuable neighbourhoods. There are no listings in the LGA, so these values are modelled from its surroundings.

Questions people ask

How much is a plot of land in Maiduguri LGA, Borno?

A typical 900 m² residential plot in Maiduguri LGA, Borno is about ₦2.5m (around ₦2,820 per m²) in asking prices as of October 2026. Commercial land is about ₦7,020 per m². Source: Buildocrat Land Value Book, updated weekly.

How much is rent for a 3-bedroom flat in Maiduguri LGA, Borno?

About ₦798,000 a year for a 3-bedroom flat, ₦604,000 for a 2-bedroom flat and ₦284,000 for a 1-bedroom or mini flat (October 2026).

How much does a 4-bedroom house cost in Maiduguri LGA, Borno?

Asking prices for a 4-bedroom house in Maiduguri LGA, Borno are around ₦69.5m; a 3-bedroom flat around ₦24.7m (October 2026).
